# Decathlon Sales Analysis | Power BI

## Project Overview
This project focuses on analyzing Decathlon's retail sales performance using Microsoft Power BI. The objective is to evaluate sales trends, identify high-performing sports categories, brands, and regions, and generate actionable business insights through interactive visualizations.

## Tools & Technologies
- Microsoft Power BI Desktop
- Power Query
- DAX

## Business Objectives
- Analyze monthly net sales and compare current-year performance with the previous year.
- Evaluate year-to-date (YTD) sales across sports categories and product brands.
- Identify regional contributions to overall net sales.
- Examine the relationship between product sales and gross margin.
- Highlight key business trends to support data-driven decisions.

## Dashboard Analysis
The Power BI dashboard covers:
1. Monthly Net Sales – Current-year vs. previous-year comparison.
2. Sports Category Performance – YTD sales across different sports.
3. Product Type Performance – Sales and gross margin analysis.
4. Regional Sales Analysis – Contribution of different regions.
5. Brand Performance – Identification of top-performing brands.

## Key Insights
- **Sales Trends:** May and August recorded the highest monthly sales in both 2025 and 2026.
- **Category Performance:** Hiking, Badminton, and Fitness collectively contributed **43.97%** of net sales in 2026.
- **Regional Performance:** Southern India accounted for **31.69%** of overall net sales, making it the leading region.
- **Top Brands:** Quechua, Perfly, and Domyos emerged as leading brands in 2026.
